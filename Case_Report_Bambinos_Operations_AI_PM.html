Deliverable B: AI & No-Code Re-Engineered Architecture Rubric Weight: 25%

1. Re-Thinking the Core Architecture: Eliminating Bad Steps

True process re-engineering does not blindly automate bad manual steps; it questions why they exist. Why is the teacher copying invoice links into Google Forms?

The Solution — Metadata-Enriched Payment Links: When a teacher pitches renewal or an automated renewal link is generated, Razorpay/Stripe metadata fields are populated with: {"student_id": 68101, "classbatch_id": 9201, "educator_id": 1102, "expected_sessions": 24}. When the parent completes payment, the webhook carries 100% of the required context directly into the system — completely eliminating Step 2 for 85%+ of renewals!

2. The "Zero-Touch" Fast-Track Workflow

1
Payment Gateway Webhook Trigger: Razorpay / Stripe captures transaction event and triggers n8n webhook with normalized JSON payload (< 1 sec).
2
Instant Master Verification: Real-time lookup against reference_active_batches_and_students to verify student enrollment, educator assignment, and batch capacity.
3
Happy-Path Rule Engine: If payment is Paid, batch capacity is available (e.g. 4/6), and student is continuing in same batch: AUTO-APPROVE INSTANTLY.
4
Automated Database Insertion: Inserts completed record in booking_requests and writes 24 session rows into class_schedule_bookings with recurring dates and timestamps.
5
Multi-Channel Dispatch: Parent receives WhatsApp confirmation with 1-click .ics calendar invite; Educator receives Slack notification with updated batch roster. Total TAT: 38 Seconds. Zero Ops touches.

Deliverable D: 30-60-90 Day Rollout & Change Management Plan Change Management

1. Executive Rollout Strategy (200+ Educators & 10 Ops Personnel)

Transforming ed-tech operations across 200+ live teachers cannot be executed via a sudden cutover ("big bang"), which risks severe scheduling disruption. We adopt a phased, risk-mitigated rollout:

Phase 1: Days 1–30 — Shadow Mode, Pilot Cohort & Calibration

Goal: Validate AI fuzzy matching, calibrate confidence scoring thresholds, and achieve 0% live class disruption.

  • Days 1–10: Dual-Run Shadow Mode: Automation runs in parallel with legacy Ops. The engine logs auto-approval recommendations without altering production tables. Discrepancies audited daily.
  • Days 11–20: Pilot Cohort Launch: Select top 20 high-volume educators (English & Maths categories). Activate live Zero-Touch for clean renewals with existing batches.
  • Days 21–30: AI Confidence Tuning: Calibrate Levenshtein thresholds for parent phone/email matching. Establish 0.85 confidence threshold for auto-approval.

Phase 2: Days 31–60 — Metadata Payment Links & Ops Reskilling

Goal: Eliminate teacher form entry for 70% of renewals and transition Ops from manual processors to Exception Managers.

  • Days 31–40: Metadata Gateway Integration: Roll out pre-populated Razorpay/Stripe renewal links carrying student_id and batch_id. Form entry eliminated for participating teachers.
  • Days 41–50: Scale Cohort to 100 Educators: Expand automation to Chess, Gita, and Science tracks across India and GCC regions.
  • Days 51–60: Ops Team Role Transition: Reskill the 10-person Ops team from repetitive copy-pasting to proactive Exception Management and capacity planning.

Phase 3: Days 61–90 — 100% Rollout, Global Regions & WhatsApp Bot

Goal: Achieve >85% overall Zero-Touch rate across all 200+ teachers, global timezones (US/UK/GCC), and self-serve parent scheduling.

  • Days 61–75: 100% Organization Rollout: Onboard remaining 100 educators. Deprecate legacy Google Form for renewals.
  • Days 76–85: Interactive WhatsApp Self-Serve Bot: Parents requesting slot changes or encountering full batches receive interactive WhatsApp buttons to self-select available batches.
  • Days 86–90: Full Operations Review: Audit KPIs with Head of Operations: Verify < 45s TAT for Happy Path, 0% Orphaned Invoices, and 140+ hours/month in labor savings.
